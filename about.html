ABOUT BLOOM’S RELAX

Making room for more beautiful moments outside.

Bloom’s Relax Lawnscaping, Inc. has served the Niagara Frontier since 1984. Based in Youngstown, New York, our business has a long history of helping local property owners imagine and improve their outdoor spaces.

From landscape design and installations to patios, water gardens, lawn establishment, and irrigation, we welcome conversations about projects big and small.

Our president is Michael C. Karbowski. We invite you to reach out to discuss your project and current service offerings.
