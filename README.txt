BLOOM’S RELAX — STATIC WEBSITE

Upload the ZIP file directly to Cloudflare Pages using Direct Upload, or upload the contents of this folder.

No WordPress, PHP, database, or build step is required.

Contact form: opens the visitor’s email program; it does NOT send or save messages on the server.

Before going live: verify contact details, current services, company history, and employment information. Replace externally hosted Unsplash photos with your own licensed business photos when possible. Fonts are served by Google Fonts; photos by Unsplash.

Custom domain: add bloomsrelax.com under your Cloudflare Pages project, then follow the DNS prompts.
